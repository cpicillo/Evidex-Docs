# Evidence and Oversight

This section explains how TRACE supports **ongoing oversight, audit readiness, and evidence production**.

TRACE is designed so governance evidence is created **as a byproduct of normal workflows**, rather than assembled retroactively in response to audits or reviews.

The guides in this section show how to:

* Review the current risk posture
* Examine historical decisions
* Access audit logs and records
* Export evidence for internal or external review

### Evidence as a System Output

In TRACE, evidence is not manually curated.

As users:

* Create and approve assessments
* Identify and decide on risks
* Review and approve mitigations
* Use AI assistance

The platform automatically records:

* What happened
* Who acted
* When actions occurred
* What information was used

This creates a continuous, defensible record of governance activity.

### Oversight Without Reconstruction

Traditional governance processes often rely on:

* Emails
* Spreadsheets
* Meeting notes
* After-the-fact explanations

TRACE is designed to reduce reliance on reconstruction.

Instead of answering:

> “What do we think happened?”

Organizations can review:

* Approved assessments
* Decision records
* Risk status
* AI audit logs
* Approval histories

All linked and time-stamped.

### Ongoing Risk Visibility

Evidence and oversight in TRACE are not limited to point-in-time reviews.

The platform supports:

* Continuous visibility into approved risks
* Tracking of mitigation status
* Review of accepted or deferred risks over time
* Historical comparison of decisions and outcomes

This enables proactive governance rather than reactive reporting.
