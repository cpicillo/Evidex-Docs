# AI Controls

This section explains how Evidex provides **explicit controls over how artificial intelligence is used** within governance workflows.

AI in Evidex is designed to assist with drafting and analysis while preserving:

* Human accountability
* Transparency of use
* Auditability of outputs
* Organizational control over models and scope

The guides in this section describe **how AI is configured, reviewed, and governed** by users — not how decisions are automated.

### AI Is Optional and Controlled

AI features in Evidex are **optional** and can be enabled, disabled, or limited based on organizational preferences.

Key characteristics of AI use in Evidex:

* AI actions are initiated by humans
* AI outputs are clearly labeled
* AI does not approve assessments, risks, or decisions
* AI never acts autonomously

Organizations remain in control of when and how AI is used.

### Separation of Assistance and Decision-Making

Evidex intentionally separates:

* **AI-assisted analysis**\
  from
* **Human approvals and decisions**

AI may assist with:

* Drafting assessment content
* Summarizing information
* Highlighting potential considerations

AI does not:

* Determine compliance
* Approve content
* Accept or reject risk
* Create decision records

This separation ensures AI enhances efficiency without undermining governance integrity.

### Visibility and Auditability

All AI-assisted activity in Evidex is:

* Logged
* Attributable to a user
* Traceable to a specific model
* Reviewable after the fact

AI usage is documented through **AI Audit Logs**, enabling organizations to explain exactly how AI contributed to governance workflows.

### Model Selection and Configuration

Organizations can:

* Select which AI model is used
* Change models over time
* Disable AI assistance entirely

Model choices are visible and auditable, ensuring alignment with internal policies and risk tolerance.

### Guides in This Section

The following guides explain how to manage AI controls in practice:

* **Selecting or Changing AI Models**
* **Reviewing AI-Generated Content**
* **Viewing AI Audit Logs**

Each guide focuses on operational use while reinforcing governance safeguards.
