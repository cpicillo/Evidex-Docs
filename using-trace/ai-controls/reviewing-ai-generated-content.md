# Reviewing AI-Generated Content

Reviewing AI-generated content ensures that **all information used in governance workflows is accurate, appropriate, and intentionally approved by humans**.

In TRACE, AI can assist with drafting or analysis, but **AI outputs are never authoritative on their own**.\
This review step confirms that AI-assisted content is suitable for use before it becomes part of an approved assessment, risk decision, or evidence record.

### When AI-Generated Content Should Be Reviewed

AI-generated content should always be reviewed:

* Immediately after generation
* Before saving content as part of an assessment
* Before submitting an assessment for approval
* Before using AI-assisted analysis in decision-making

AI-generated content must never be approved without human review.

### How to Review AI-Generated Content

#### Step 1: Locate AI-Generated Sections

Open the assessment or analysis where AI assistance was used.

AI-generated content appears inline within the relevant section and is clearly marked.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/AI Content REcommendation.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

#### Step 2: Validate Accuracy and Context

Review the content to confirm:

* Factual accuracy
* Alignment with the specific system, vendor, or process
* Consistency with organizational policies
* Appropriate level of specificity

AI output should be treated as a **draft suggestion**, not a final answer.

#### Step 3: Edit or Refine the Content

Users may:

* Edit language for clarity or precision
* Add missing context
* Remove incorrect or irrelevant statements
* Replace AI-generated content entirely

All edits are tracked through normal version history.
