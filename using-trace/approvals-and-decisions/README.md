# Approvals and Decisions

Approvals and decisions in TRACE are designed to ensure that:

* Governance outcomes are explicit
* Responsibility is clearly attributed
* Decisions are supported by documented inputs
* Records can be reviewed and defended later

Rather than relying on informal sign-offs or external documentation, TRACE captures approvals and decisions directly within the platform.

### How Approvals and Decisions Work Together

In TRACE, approvals and decisions serve different but related purposes:

* **Approvals** validate information
* **Decisions** determine action

An assessment must be approved before it can be used to:

* Identify risks
* Create decision records
* Enter risks into the risk register

This separation ensures that decisions are made using **validated inputs**, not preliminary analysis.

### Decision Records as the Source of Truth

Decisions in TRACE are recorded explicitly.

A decision record documents:

* What decision was made
* Who made the decision
* When the decision occurred
* What information was considered
* Any conditions or rationale provided

Decision records prevent ambiguity and eliminate the need to reconstruct reasoning after the fact.

### Human Accountability by Design

TRACE does not automate approvals or decisions.

In TRACE:

* Approvals are initiated and completed by humans
* Decisions are selected and confirmed by humans
* AI may assist with analysis, but never finalizes outcomes

This ensures accountability is **direct, reviewable, and defensible**.

### Traceability Across Governance Workflows

Approvals and decisions are linked to:

* The originating assessment
* Identified risks
* Mitigation actions
* The risk register

This creates a continuous audit trail from assessment through decision and ongoing oversight.

### What Gets Recorded

Across approval and decision workflows, TRACE records:

* Submissions and approvals
* Decision selections and rationale
* User attribution
* Timestamps and status changes
* Any AI-assisted activity related to analysis

These records support audits, regulatory reviews, and internal governance oversight.
