# Creating Decision Records

Creating a decision record documents **how an organization chose to act** based on approved assessments and identified risks.

Decision records in TRACE capture:

* The decision made
* The rationale behind the decision
* The responsible decision-maker
* The supporting information considered

This ensures decisions are **intentional, attributable, and reviewable**.

### When to Create a Decision Record

A decision record should be created when:

* An assessment has been approved
* Risks have been identified
* A determination must be made on how to proceed

Common examples include decisions to:

* Accept risk
* Mitigate risk
* Transfer risk
* Avoid risk

Decision records prevent ambiguity by making outcomes explicit.

### How Decision Records Work in TRACE

Decision records are created **after approval**, not during drafting.

Key characteristics:

* Decisions are initiated by a human user
* Decisions are explicitly selected
* Decisions require confirmation
* No decisions are made automatically

This ensures accountability is clear and auditable.

### Creating a Decision Record

#### Step 1: Start from an Approved Assessment or Risk

Decision records are typically created from:

* An approved assessment
* An identified risk linked to an approved assessment

Only validated inputs can be used to create decisions.

#### Step 2: Select the Decision Outcome

Choose the appropriate decision type, such as:

* Accept risk
* Mitigate risk
* Transfer risk
* Avoid risk

The selected outcome determines how the risk will be tracked and managed.

#### Step 3: Document Decision Rationale

Provide context for the decision, including:

* Why this option was selected
* Key considerations or trade-offs
* Any conditions or limitations

Clear rationale supports future review and audit.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Risk Decisdion.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

#### Step 4: Confirm and Record the Decision

Once confirmed:

* The decision record is finalized
* The decision is linked to the relevant assessment and risk
* The decision becomes part of the system of record

Decision records cannot be silently altered after creation.

### What TRACE Records

For each decision record, TRACE captures:

* The decision outcome
* The rationale provided
* The decision-maker
* The date and time of the decision
* Links to supporting assessments and risks
* Any AI-assisted analysis used prior to the decision

This creates a defensible decision trail.

### Relationship to the Risk Register

Once created:

* Decision records update the status of associated risks
* Risks are tracked according to the selected outcome
* Mitigation decisions may generate follow-up actions

The risk register reflects **decisions made**, not assumptions.
