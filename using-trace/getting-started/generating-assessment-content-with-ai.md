# Generating Assessment Content with AI

What This Accomplishes

TRACE allows users to **optionally use AI to assist with drafting assessment content**.

AI assistance is designed to:

* Help summarize information
* Improve clarity and consistency
* Identify potentially relevant considerations

AI does **not** make decisions, approve assessments, or finalize content.\
All AI-generated content remains subject to human review and approval.

### When to Use AI Assistance

Users commonly use AI assistance when:

* Drafting initial assessment responses
* Summarizing complex or lengthy inputs
* Normalizing language across assessment sections
* Identifying potential risks or gaps to review

AI assistance is optional and can be used selectively on a per-section basis.

### How AI Assistance Works in TRACE

AI assistance in TRACE is **explicit, controlled, and traceable**.

Key characteristics:

* AI actions are initiated by a human user
* AI-generated content is clearly labeled
* Outputs are suggestions, not final answers
* Content can be edited, rejected, or replaced by users

AI is integrated to support judgment — not replace it.

### How to Generate Content with AI

#### Step 1: Open an Assessment Section

Navigate to the assessment you are working on and open the relevant section.

AI assistance is available only within supported sections and only while the assessment is in draft status.

#### Step 2: Initiate AI Assistance

Select the option to generate or assist with content.

Depending on the section, AI may:

* Draft a response based on provided inputs
* Summarize existing text
* Suggest considerations or risks for review

AI assistance is clearly indicated before generation occurs.

#### Step 3: Review AI-Generated Content

After generation, the AI output is displayed inline and clearly labeled as AI-generated.

Users can:

* Edit the content
* Accept parts of the content
* Discard the content entirely

AI-generated content is **never applied automatically**.

#### Step 4: Save Changes

Once reviewed and edited, users may save the content as part of the assessment draft.

Saving AI-assisted content does not approve the assessment or create a decision record.

### What TRACE Records

For every AI-assisted action, TRACE creates a corresponding **AI audit log entry**, including:

* The purpose of the AI action
* The section where AI was used
* A summary of input information
* A preview of the AI output
* The AI provider and model used
* The initiating user
* Timestamps and integrity metadata

These records support traceability and auditability without relying on inference.

### Human Review and Approval

AI-generated content does not bypass TRACE’s approval workflow.

Before any assessment content:

* Becomes approved
* Triggers risk identification
* Is used in decision-making

It must be reviewed and approved by authorized users.

Human accountability remains explicit at all times.

### Limits and Responsibilities

AI assistance in TRACE:

* Does not provide legal advice
* Does not determine compliance
* Does not approve content or decisions

Organizations remain responsible for reviewing AI-assisted content and ensuring it is accurate, appropriate, and complete.
