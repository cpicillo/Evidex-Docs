# Audit Center

The Audit Center is a consolidated view of evidence, decisions, and risk history designed to support audits and internal reviews.

It provides traceability from:

* assessment → risk → decision → outcome

#### What Auditors Can See

* When assessments were completed
* What risks were identified
* How risks were handled
* Who approved decisions

This reduces audit preparation time and manual evidence gathering.
