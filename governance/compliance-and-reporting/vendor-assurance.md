# Vendor Assurance

Vendor Assurance provides an aggregated view of third-party risk based on real assessments — not static questionnaires.

Vendor risk posture is derived from:

* linked assessments
* identified risks
* AI and compliance indicators

#### What You Can See

* Vendor-level risk posture
* Assessments associated with each vendor
* Open and historical risks
* Pending or completed decisions

#### Why It’s Different

Vendor Assurance in Evidex is:

* continuous
* assessment-backed
* decision-driven

Vendor risk evolves as assessments evolve.
