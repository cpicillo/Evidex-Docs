# Approving or Rejecting Risks

Approving or rejecting risks formally determines **how identified risks are treated by the organization**.

In Evidex, risk approval is not implicit.\
Each risk must be explicitly reviewed and either approved, rejected, or returned for further analysis.

This ensures that risk handling decisions are **intentional, documented, and defensible**.

### When Risks Are Reviewed

Risks are reviewed after:

* An assessment has been approved
* Risks have been identified and documented
* A decision record has been created or proposed

Only validated, documented risks can move forward to approval.

### How Risk Approval Works in Evidex

Risk approval workflows in Evidex are:

* Human-initiated
* Role-based
* Fully auditable

Key characteristics:

* Risks cannot be silently accepted or dismissed
* Approval outcomes are explicitly recorded
* Rejected risks remain documented, not deleted

This prevents undocumented risk treatment.

### Approving a Risk

#### Step 1: Review Risk Details

Before approving, reviewers should confirm:

* The risk description is accurate
* The risk source is clearly identified
* Supporting assessments are complete
* Any AI-assisted analysis has been reviewed

#### Step 2: Review the Proposed Decision

Review the associated decision record, including:

* Selected risk treatment (accept, mitigate, transfer, avoid)
* Rationale provided
* Any conditions or follow-up actions

Approvers validate the decision, not just the risk itself.

#### Step 3: Approve the Risk

When approved:

* The risk status is updated accordingly
* The decision becomes authoritative
* The risk enters the risk register under the approved disposition

### Rejecting or Returning a Risk

Risks may be rejected or returned when:

* Information is incomplete
* Assumptions require clarification
* The proposed treatment is not acceptable

When a risk is rejected:

* The rejection is recorded
* Feedback is visible to the risk owner
* The risk remains documented for traceability

Rejected risks are not erased — they remain part of the record.

### What Evidex Records

For each risk approval or rejection, Evidex records:

* The approver’s identity
* The approval or rejection outcome
* Any comments or conditions provided
* Timestamps for review and action
* Links to related assessments and decision records

This ensures a clear chain of responsibility.

### Impact on the Risk Register

Once approved:

* Risks appear in the risk register with their approved status
* Accepted risks remain monitored
* Mitigated risks generate follow-up actions
* Rejected risks remain archived for reference

The risk register reflects **approved risk positions**, not preliminary analysis.
