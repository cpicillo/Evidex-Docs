# Submitting an Assessment for Approval

Submitting an assessment for approval signals that the assessment content is **complete and ready for formal review**.

Approval establishes the assessment as a **validated organizational position** and enables downstream workflows such as:

* Risk identification
* Risk decision-making
* Mitigation planning
* Audit and regulatory evidence

Until an assessment is approved, its content remains draft and cannot be used to create decisions of record.

### When to Submit an Assessment

An assessment should be submitted for approval when:

* All required sections are complete
* Content has been reviewed for accuracy
* Any AI-assisted content has been validated
* Open questions or gaps have been resolved

Submitting incomplete assessments may delay approval or require revisions.

### How the Approval Process Works

Evidex uses **explicit, human-driven approval workflows**.

Key characteristics:

* Approvals are initiated by a user
* Approvers are clearly identified
* Approval outcomes are recorded
* No automatic approvals occur

This ensures that responsibility for validation is clear and auditable.

### How to Submit an Assessment for Approval

#### Step 1: Confirm Assessment Readiness

Before submission, review the assessment to ensure:

* All sections are complete
* Responses are clear and accurate
* Supporting information is included where appropriate

Assessments must be in **draft** status to be submitted.

#### Step 2: Initiate Approval

From the assessment view, select **Submit for Approval**.

Depending on your organization’s configuration, you may:

* Select one or more approvers
* Add optional comments or context for reviewer

#### Step 3: Review Status and Notifications

Once submitted:

* The assessment status updates to **Pending Approval**
* Selected approvers are notified
* The assessment becomes read-only for most users

This prevents changes while the assessment is under review.

#### Step 4: Respond to Feedback (if required)

Approvers may:

* Approve the assessment
* Request changes
* Reject the assessment with comments

If changes are requested:

* The assessment returns to draft status
* Feedback is visible to the owner
* Revisions can be made before resubmission

### What Evidex Records

When an assessment is submitted and reviewed, Evidex records:

* The user who submitted the assessment
* The approver(s) assigned
* Approval status and outcomes
* Timestamps for submission and approval
* Any comments or conditions provided

These records create a clear audit trail for validation and accountability.

### What Happens After Approval

Once approved:

* The assessment becomes a validated record
* Risk identification workflows may be initiated
* Approved content can be used to create decision records
* The assessment can be referenced in audits and reports

Approved assessments cannot be modified without creating a new version.
