# Editing and Reviewing Assessment Content

Editing and reviewing assessment content ensures that all information documented in Evidex is **accurate, complete, and ready for approval**.

This step is where users validate:

* Factual correctness
* Completeness of responses
* Alignment with organizational policies
* Appropriateness of any AI-assisted content

Assessments cannot be approved or used for decision-making until this review is complete.

### When to Review Assessment Content

Assessment content should be reviewed:

* After initial drafting
* After using AI assistance
* When material changes are made
* Before submitting the assessment for approval

Reviewing content before approval helps prevent delays, rework, and incomplete decision records.

### How Editing Works in Evidex

Assessment content in Evidex remains editable while the assessment is in **draft** status.

During this stage:

* Users can edit any section
* Changes are saved as new versions
* Previous content is preserved in version history

This supports collaborative drafting without losing traceability.

### Reviewing Assessment Sections

#### Step 1: Review Each Section for Completeness

Navigate through each assessment section and confirm that:

* All required fields are populated
* Responses are clear and specific
* Assumptions are documented where applicable

Incomplete sections may prevent approval or trigger follow-up requests.

#### Step 2: Validate AI-Assisted Content

If AI assistance was used, review the generated content carefully.

Confirm that:

* The content accurately reflects the system or activity
* No assumptions or inaccuracies were introduced
* The language aligns with organizational standards

AI-generated content should be treated as a draft.

#### Step 3: Edit and Refine Content

Users may:

* Revise language for clarity
* Add missing details
* Remove irrelevant or incorrect information

Edits can be made directly within each section.

#### Step 4: Collaborate and Iterate

Multiple users may contribute to an assessment while it remains in draft status.

This allows:

* Subject matter experts to add input
* Reviewers to request clarifications
* Owners to finalize content before approval

All edits are captured through version history.

### What Evidex Records

During editing and review, Evidex records:

* Who made changes
* When changes were made
* Version history of assessment content
* Any associated AI-assisted activity

These records support transparency and audit readiness.

### Review Best Practices

To support efficient approvals:

* Ensure all sections are complete
* Resolve open questions before submission
* Avoid informal or ambiguous language
* Clearly distinguish facts from assumptions

Well-reviewed assessments reduce back-and-forth during approval.
