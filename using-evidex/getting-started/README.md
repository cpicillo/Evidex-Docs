# Getting Started

Rather than acting as a simple documentation tool, Evidex connects assessments, approvals, risk identification, decision-making, and evidence into a single, traceable workflow.

The guides in this section focus on the **first actions users typically take** and establish the foundation for all downstream governance activities.

### How Workflows in Evidex Are Structured

Evidex workflows are intentionally sequential and controlled.

Most users begin by:

1. Creating an assessment
2. Drafting and reviewing assessment content (optionally with AI assistance)
3. Submitting the assessment for approval
4. Reviewing identified risks
5. Making and recording risk decisions

Each step builds on the previous one and is designed to ensure that decisions are **explicit, reviewable, and attributable**.

### Human Review and Accountability

Evidex is built around human-in-the-loop governance.

While AI may assist with drafting or analysis:

* All actions are initiated by a human
* AI outputs are clearly labeled
* No content becomes a decision of record without human approval

This ensures that governance outcomes remain explainable and defensible.

### Records and Auditability

As users complete workflows in Evidex, the platform automatically creates records that support traceability and audit readiness.

This includes:

* Assessment drafts and approvals
* Risk identification and treatment decisions
* AI-assisted activity (where used)
* User actions and timestamps

These records are maintained to support internal reviews, audits, and regulatory inquiries.

### What to Do First

If you are new to Evidex, start with the following guides in order:

1. **Creating an Assessment**
2. **Generating Assessment Content with AI** (optional)
3. **Editing and Reviewing Assessment Content**
4. **Submitting an Assessment for Approval**

Completing these steps establishes a validated assessment that can be used for risk identification, decision-making, and ongoing oversight.

### Next Steps

Once an assessment has been approved, users typically proceed to:

* Reviewing identified risks
* Recording risk decisions
* Managing risks and mitigations over time

These workflows are covered in the **Approvals and Decisions** and **Controls and Risk Management** sections.
