# Generating Technical and Organizational Measures (TOMs)

Generating Technical and Organizational Measures (TOMs) in Evidex allows organizations to **document safeguards designed to address identified risks**.

TOMs describe the technical and organizational actions an organization takes to:

* Prevent, detect, or mitigate risk
* Protect personal data and systems
* Demonstrate compliance with contractual and regulatory expectations

TOMs are derived from approved assessments, controls, and risks, and must be **reviewed and approved by humans** before being relied upon.

### How TOM Generation Works in Evidex

TOM generation in Evidex is:

* **Context-driven** (based on approved inputs)
* **Optional AI-assisted**
* **Human-reviewed and approved**

Evidex does not automatically declare risks mitigated based on TOMs alone.\
Mitigation effectiveness must still be reviewed and approved.

### How to Generate Technical and Organizational Measures

#### Step 1: Start from an Identified Control&#x20;

TOMs are typically generated from:

* Controls implemented by the organization based on a scope (i.e., the production environment or an application having SOC 2 certification0
* A set of related controls within the organization

Only risks that have been documented, accepted, and reviewed can be used to generate TOMs.

#### Step 2: Review Applicable Controls and Context

Before generating TOMs, review:

* Related internal controls
* The nature and impact of the risk
* Regulatory or contractual expectations

This ensures TOMs are relevant and appropriately scoped.

#### Step 3: Generate Draft TOMs (Optional AI Assistance)

Users may optionally use AI assistance to:

* Draft structured TOM language
* Normalize safeguards across risks
* Improve clarity and consistency

AI-generated TOMs are clearly labeled and treated as **drafts only**.

#### Step 4: Review and Edit TOMs

All TOMs must be reviewed by a human to:

* Confirm accuracy and applicability
* Remove generic or inapplicable safeguards
* Add organization-specific detail
* TOMs are exportable to word

TOMs should reflect **actual safeguards**, not aspirational ones.

### What Evidex Records

When TOMs are generated and reviewed, Evidex records:

* The risks and assessments used as inputs
* Whether AI assistance was used
* The user(s) who reviewed and edited TOMs
* Approval status and timestamps

These records support traceability and audit readiness.

### Important Notes and Limitations

* TOMs are not legal advice
* TOMs do not guarantee compliance
* TOMs must reflect implemented or planned safeguards
* Organizations remain responsible for contractual commitments

Evidex provides tooling, not certification.
