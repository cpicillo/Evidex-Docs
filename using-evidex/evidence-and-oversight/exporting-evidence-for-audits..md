# Exporting Evidence for Audits.

Exporting evidence in Evidex allows organizations to **share structured, verifiable records** with auditors, regulators, and internal reviewers.

Exports are intended to support:

* Audit preparation
* Regulatory inquiries
* Internal reviews and investigations
* Executive or board reporting

Evidex exports reflect **records that already exist within the platform**.\
They do not modify, summarize, or reinterpret decisions.

### When to Export Evidence

Organizations typically export evidence when:

* Preparing for internal or external audits
* Responding to regulatory questions
* Supporting vendor, customer, or procurement reviews
* Escalating issues to leadership or oversight committees

Exports provide a snapshot of evidence at a point in time.

### Types of Evidence That Can Be Exported

Depending on configuration and permissions, users may export:

* Approved assessments
* Decision records and rationales
* Risk register entries
* Mitigation status and ownership
* Approval histories
* AI audit metadata (where applicable)

Only approved and recorded items are included in exports.

### How Evidence Exports Work in Evidex

#### Step 1: Select the Scope of Evidence

Users can choose which records to export, such as:

* Specific assessments
* A subset of risks
* A defined time period
* Records related to a particular system or vendor

This ensures exports are targeted and relevant.

#### Step 2: Generate the Export

Once the scope is defined:

* Evidex generates a structured export
* Records retain their original timestamps and attribution
* Relationships between assessments, decisions, and risks are preserved

Exports may be generated in formats suitable for review and archiving.

#### Step 3: Review and Share

Generated exports can be:

* Reviewed internally
* Shared securely with auditors or reviewers
* Archived as part of audit workpapers

Exports do not alter underlying records.

### What Evidex Records

When evidence is exported, Evidex records:

* Who generated the export
* When the export occurred
* What records were included
* The scope and parameters used

This ensures that evidence sharing itself is auditable.

### Integrity and Traceability

Exports from Evidex are designed to preserve:

* Original decision context
* Approval history
* User attribution
* Chronological order

Evidence remains traceable back to its source within the platform.

### Limitations and Responsibilities

Evidence exports:

* Do not constitute legal advice
* Do not certify compliance
* Do not replace professional judgment

Organizations remain responsible for interpreting and presenting evidence in accordance with applicable requirements.
