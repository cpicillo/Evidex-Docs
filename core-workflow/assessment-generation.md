# Assessment Generation

Assessment Generation is the starting point of TRACE. It allows teams to generate structured, framework-aligned assessments that identify privacy, security, and AI governance risks across systems, processes, and vendors.

Assessments are not static questionnaires, they are **risk discovery mechanisms** that feed directly into the Risk Workspace.

#### What You Can Do

* Generate assessments aligned to regulatory and governance frameworks
* Use AI-assisted guidance to accelerate completion
* Capture contextual details about systems, vendors, and use cases
* Automatically identify risks based on responses
*   #### What Happens Next

    Once an assessment is completed:

    * Risks are identified and structured
    * Risks flow automatically into the Risk Workspace
    * Linked entities (e.g., vendors, systems, AI use cases) are preserved

    Assessments are the **source of truth** for all downstream risk and compliance workflows.
