# Risk Workspace

The Risk Workspace is where identified risks are reviewed, evaluated, and managed over time.

It centralizes all risks identified through assessments and provides the tools needed to make defensible, auditable decisions.

#### Core Components

* **Risk Register** – a system of record for all identified risks
* **Decision Window** – structured workflows for accepting, mitigating, or escalating risk
* **Status tracking** – open, accepted, mitigated, or retired risks

#### Why It Matters

The Risk Workspace turns assessment output into:

* actionable decisions
* documented accountability
* auditable evidence

This is the operational heart of TRACE.
