# How Decisions Work in Evidex

Evidex is designed to ensure that **every governance decision can be explained, justified, and defended**.

Rather than focusing solely on documentation, Evidex provides a structured workflow that connects:

* Assessments
* Human approvals
* Risk identification
* Decision-making
* Mitigation actions
* Ongoing risk management

This creates a **single, traceable decision record** — not disconnected artifacts.

#### Step 1: Structured Assessments

Decisions in Evidex begin with structured assessments.

Organizations use standardized templates to document:

* Privacy impact assessments (DPIAs / PIAs)
* AI system assessments
* Article 30 Reports
* CCPA Risk Assessments
* Data transfers assessments

Assessments are completed intentionally and remain **drafts** until reviewed and approved.

No downstream actions occur based on unapproved content.

#### Step 2: Human Review and Approval

Before any decision is derived, assessments must be explicitly approved by authorized users. End users can create approvers by adding their information into the application. Once selected to approve an assessment, they will receive an email with the assessment details. The approver can also use AI to summarize the full assessment to aid in the review process. All AI usage is logged.

Approval records include:

* Who approved the assessment
* When approval occurred
* Any required comments or conditions

This ensures that assessments represent a **validated organizational position**, not preliminary analysis.

#### Step 3: Risk Identification

Once an assessment is approved, Evidex identifies potential risks based on the approved content.

Risk identification:

* Occurs only after approval
* Is based on documented assessment inputs
* Produces discrete, reviewable risk items

AI may assist in identifying potential risks, but **risk identification does not equal risk acceptance**.

All identified risks require human review.&#x20;

Risk approvals follow the same approval workflow as assessments where the approver will receive an email to approve or reject a risk.&#x20;

#### Step 4: Risk Decision-Making

Each identified risk enters a decision workflow.

Authorized users  must explicitly decide how the risk will be handled, typically by selecting one of the following treatments:

* Accept
* Mitigate&#x20;
* Transfer
* Avoid

For every risk decision, Evidex records:

* The selected treatment
* The decision rationale
* The decision-maker
* The timestamp of the decision

No risk moves forward without a documented human decision.

#### Step 5: Mitigation and Conditions

When risks are mitigated, Evidex captures:

* Required mitigation measures
  * The end user can use AI to suggest mitigating measures.
* Assigned ownership
* Supporting documentation

Mitigations must be reviewed and approved before a risk is considered resolved or accepted under conditions.

This prevents informal or undocumented risk handling.

#### Step 6: Risk Register as the System of Record

Approved risks are recorded in the Risk Register, which serves as the **system of record**.

The Risk Register:

* Reflects current, approved risk posture
* Maintains links to originating assessments and decisions
* Tracks mitigation status over time

This ensures risks are managed as ongoing obligations, not one-time events.

#### Traceability Across the Entire Lifecycle

Evidex maintains end-to-end traceability across the full decision lifecycle:

Assessment → Approval → Risk Identification → Decision → Mitigation → Risk Register

For every step, Evidex records:

* Who acted
* What was decided
* When it occurred
* What information was used

AI involvement, where applicable, is logged and auditable via AI Audit Logs.

#### Human Accountability by Design

Evidex does not make decisions.

In Evidex:

* Humans initiate actions
* Humans review and approve content
* Humans decide how risks are treated
* Humans remain accountable for outcomes

The platform exists to **document, structure, and preserve** those decisions not to replace judgment.&#x20;

Evidex turns governance decisions into durable, auditable records, ensuring accountability is preserved even as teams, systems, and technologies change.
